# Design System

## Visual Thesis

The interface is a pedagogical planning workbench. Bloom levels behave as physical tool trays: ordered, inspectable and ready to turn verbs into observable evidence. It avoids generic educational cards and literal staircase imagery.

## Color

- Deep navy `#082746`: primary work surface and high-rigor sections.
- Ink navy `#102A43`: text and control chrome.
- Institutional blue `#0B63A1`: actions, selected states and lower-order trays.
- Teal `#087E84`: comprehension and secondary signals.
- Deep orange `#B84A00`: analysis and transformation; chosen to preserve white-text contrast.
- Signal yellow `#F7C516`: primary actions, focus and time-protection guidance.
- Paper `#F4F5F6` and white: reading surfaces and visual rest.

Color communicates state at large scale, but selection also uses position, outline and labels.

## Typography

- Antonio: condensed structural headings, labels and controls.
- Bahianita: opening display statement only, matching the compressed lettering of the approved composition.
- Source Sans 3: body copy and instructional text.

Headings are direct, uppercase and compact. Body copy remains sentence case with short readable measures.

## Geometry

Panels are rectangular with fine rules and occasional clipped-label cues. Rounded geometry is reserved for sequence markers and answer labels. Blueprint registration marks and calibration lines appear only on the opening workbench.

## Components

- Bloom trays: colored, ascending controls with a numbered label, line icon and verb stack. The active tray lifts, brightens and gains a yellow focus outline.
- Section headings: oversized condensed statement paired with one explanatory paragraph.
- Level explorer: six-tab instrument rail feeding one shared detail panel.
- Time shield: full-width yellow operational guidance attached to each cognitive level.
- Case stage: neutral limiting case, directional pivot and navy transforming case.
- Challenge choices: large single-select controls with immediate status feedback.

## Motion

Motion is functional and brief. Selecting a tray lifts it before the interface transitions to its detailed panel. Smooth scrolling supports continuity and is disabled when reduced motion is preferred.

## Responsive Rules

Desktop uses asymmetrical workbench compositions and side-by-side comparisons. Tablet stacks primary regions while retaining the tab rail. Mobile converts the six trays to a two-column instrument grid, collapses panels into one reading sequence and stacks comparisons vertically. No horizontal interaction is required.

## Accessibility

All interactions use native buttons or links, visible focus, semantic tab relationships, keyboard arrow navigation, status announcements and reduced-motion support. Text contrast targets WCAG AA; color is never the sole selected-state indicator.
